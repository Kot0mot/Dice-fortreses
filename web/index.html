How to Play

1) Goal

Break the enemy core before they break yours.

2) Dice and Slots

Each die goes to Build, Fortify, or Arm and powers that phase.

3) What Each Phase Does

Roll and choose hand, commit slots, build/repair, fire Arm, then end turn.

4) First Match Strategy

Build around your core first, keep Fortify stocked, and focus Arm on one column.
